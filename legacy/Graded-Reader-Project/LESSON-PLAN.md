## The plan: 2 weeks, 6 sessions (~25–30 min each)

### Week 1 — Stop the lookup loop

**Session 1 — Activate retrieval (SRS kickoff)**
- Seed the app's SRS with the Tier-1 list below (words with 3+ lookups).
- Drill Korean → English first; once a word is easy, flip to English → Korean.
- Goal: 아침/일어나다/가다/하다/있다 should never need a lookup again.

**Session 2 — Irregular conjugation crash course**
Learn these four rules, then deconjugate forms *from your own lookup history*:
| Rule | Verbs from your list | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ㅂ-irregular: ㅂ→우 before vowel | 춥다, 덥다, 반갑다, 아름답다 | 춥다 → 추워, 추운, 추우니까 |
| ㄷ-irregular: ㄷ→ㄹ before vowel | 걷다, 듣다 | 걷다 → 걸어, 걸으면서, 걸으며 |
| ㅡ-drop before 아/어 | 뜨다, 쓰다, 바쁘다, 예쁘다 | 뜨다 → 떠, 떴습니다 |
| 르-irregular: 르→ㄹ라/ㄹ러 | 부르다, 빠르다 | 부르다 → 불러 |

Self-test: cover the right column and produce 추워 / 걸으면서 / 떴어요 / 불러요 from the base forms.

**Session 3 — Connective endings (the glue you kept tripping on)**
These four appeared throughout your passages and lookups:
- **-(으)면서 / -(으)며** — "while doing" (걸으면서 = while walking)
- **-(으)니까** — "because/since" (날씨가 좋으니까 = since the weather is nice)
- **-지만** — "but" (덥지만 = it's hot, but)
- **-아/어서** — "and so / because" (늦어서 = because I was late)

Activity: re-read passages #15 and #17 in the app and find every instance of these four endings. Use the app's grammar-hint clicks to confirm.

### Week 2 — Particles, then level up

**Session 4 — Particle separation**
- 은/는 (topic) vs 이/가 (subject) — the contrast, not just the forms
- 에 (at/to, static) vs 에서 (at/from, action location)
- 까지 (until/up to) and 부터 (from)
- 한테/에게 (to a person)
- Activity: take 10 sentences from your saved passages and bracket every particle: `[저]는 [공원]에서 [친구]를 만나요`.

**Session 5 — First full intermediate passage, new topic**
Generate an intermediate passage in the app on a topic *outside* the daily-routine domain. Good candidates (they force new grammar):
- A phone call making weekend plans → future tense, -(으)ㄹ까요?, -기로 했다
- Describing last weekend's trip → past tense throughout
- Giving someone directions → imperatives, -(으)세요
- Target: finish with **fewer than 10 lookups**. Every lookup of a conjugated form: deconjugate it yourself first, then check.

**Session 6 — Output (reverse the direction)**
- Write 6–8 sentences of your own describing tomorrow's plans, using at least 8 Tier-1 words and at least 3 of the Week-1 connectives.
- Paste it into the app's refine feature and compare the native-style rewrite against yours — the diffs are your next lessons.

### Daily habit (between sessions)
10 minutes of SRS review. This matters more than the sessions themselves — the May 27 → June 10 gap is what reset your top-20 words.

## Data hygiene (15 min, optional but recommended)

Your dictionary has some wrong AI-generated definitions that will sabotage SRS if you drill them as-is:
- **하다** — stored as "To go; to arrive at a destination" → should be "to do"
- **추다** — stored as "to push; to urge" → in your passages it's "to dance" (춤을 추다)
- **동안** — stored as "Refined appearance" → should be "during; for (a period)"
- **학교** — "No results found" → school
- **제의** — stored as "one's own" → should be "proposal, suggestion"
- **이사동까지** — hallucinated place; the real neighborhood is **인사동** (Insadong)
- Garbage rows worth deleting: 그리近些데, 깽니다, 떴습니다, 그곳은, 여기는, 거기서 (inflected/fused duplicates of words you already have)
