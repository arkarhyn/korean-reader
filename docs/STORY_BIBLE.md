Read before generating any episode. Continuity rules here override

"Canon log" by the generator session.

## Premise
이선 (Ethan), a non-Korean with a Japanese-language background, has been
dating 강서윤 for a year. Her parents speak English fine, but he wants to
talk with them in Korean -- and Seoyun, who understands Korean but freezes
when speaking it, is quietly trying to get better too. Seoyun's older
sister 서현 is getting married out of state, and Ethan is going as part of
the family. Season 1 runs from the first family dinner to the wedding day:
meeting relatives and family friends, and learning what to say to whom.
The ceremony itself is in English; Korean lives at the edges -- the
parents, older relatives, family friends at the reception.
Season 2 is a family trip to Florida (Disney World or a Disney cruise).

## Setting (decided)
A Korean-American suburb outside Washington, DC. Parents' house ~30 min
from Ethan's apartment. Korean grocery, restaurants, and a dense Korean
community nearby, but daily life is bilingual.

## Register map (core teaching thread)
- Ethan -> parents: starts out avoiding direct address entirely (true to
  life). Ep 7: Seoyun tells him 어머님 / 아버님 is right for a girlfriend's
  parents, and that her mom's "아빠" means "your dad" (said to the kids) --
  he must not copy it. Ep 10: he says 어머님 naturally for the first time.
  Throughout: -(으)시-; humble verbs (드리다, 말씀드리다, 여쭤보다, 뵙다);
  honorific nouns (진지, 댁, 연세, 성함).
- Parents -> Ethan: "이선 씨" at first -> "이선아" by the finale (marks acceptance).
- Ethan <-> Seoyun: 반말 with softening endings (-는데, -거든, -잖아, -지).
- Ethan <-> 준수: 형 / 반말.
- Ethan <-> 서현: she is younger than him, so not 누나. Settles on 서현 씨 in Ep 7
  (준수 jokes he should practice 처형 -- what a husband calls his wife's older sister).
- Parents' friends / older relatives -> Ethan: polite curiosity; Ethan -> them: full 해요체/합니다체.
- Parents <-> each other: natural middle-aged spousal 반말 (side episodes only).
- Grandmother: Ethan's hardest register; mistakes are gently corrected.

## Side series
- **부모님 이야기 (parent POV):** e.g. after Ep 1, the parents talk about Ethan in
  bed ("그 사람 괜찮더라"). Natural spousal 반말 -- listening practice for how
  they actually talk.
- **옛날이야기 (folktales):** public-domain tales retold at level (흥부와 놀부,
  해와 달이 된 오누이, 금도끼 은도끼). Grandmother tells one on a call (Ep 6 tie-in).

## Tone
Warmth first, light comedy second. Humor comes from language slips and
family texture, never at a character's expense for long.

## Rules for the generator
- Never change established facts (names, ages, jobs, relationships) without a
  DECISIONS entry.
- Every episode tags its register(s) and lists any new canon in the Canon log.
- Japanese-parallel moments: at most one per episode, and they must be
  correct about where the parallel breaks.
- No dialect (사투리) or 사극 register. Grandmother speaks standard Seoul Korean.
- Vary days and times of day (the first S01E001-005 drafts all fell on 토요일 because it was the
  only weekday word known). Use the known set from the generation context.
- Humor comes from situations and language slips, never from ethnic stereotypes.
