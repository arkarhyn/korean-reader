# STORY_BIBLE (seed -- finish in a Project design session before Stage 5)

## Premise (approved direction)
A non-Korean protagonist with a Japanese-language background is learning
Korean. His girlfriend is Korean-American: she understands Korean but is
rusty speaking it and is also working on it. They start having regular
dinners and outings with her parents. Family-centered slice-of-life.

## Locked
- Protagonist loosely echoes Austin; fictional name and details.
- Cast is fictional. Real anticipated situations may be fed in as episode
  prompts (e.g. "her dad asks about my job").
- Register switching is a core thread:
  - Protagonist <-> girlfriend: 반말, softening endings (-는데, -거든, -잖아).
  - Protagonist -> parents: 해요체 / 합니다체, -(으)시-, humble verbs
    (드리다, 말씀드리다, 여쭤보다), honorific nouns (진지, 댁, 연세).
  - Parents -> protagonist: warm, natural middle-aged speech.
- Japanese-parallel moments allowed in-story (he reaches for a Japanese
  structure; someone gives the natural Korean).
- Formats: narration, dialogue, KakaoTalk-style chats, diary, phone calls,
  holidays (추석, 설날), cooking, small misunderstandings.
- Side series: parent-POV episodes; folktale side stories.
