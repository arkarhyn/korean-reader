# LEARNER_PROFILE

Read before generating ANY content (episodes, lesson cards, drills, questions).

## Level
- Grammar ahead of vocabulary. Grammar roughly TOPIK 3 / intermediate-low;
  vocabulary is the main bottleneck.
- HowToStudyKorean: jumped around Lessons 1-28, currently around Lesson 29
  (-기 / -(으)ㅁ nominalization). Skipped HTSK vocab sections; vocab comes
  from immersion.
- Placement (Stage 4) supersedes this section once run.

## Background
- Strong Japanese (reading ~N2, DLPT 2+/2 reading/listening). Use Japanese
  as the scaffold: grammar parallels, Sino-Korean <-> kanji mappings,
  pragmatics comparisons.
- Prefers Korean-medium or Japanese-primary explanations over English-only.
- Known learner trap: -지만 taught as けど; -는데 is the truer conversational
  match. Show where parallels break, not just where they hold.

## Goal
- Conversational fluency and natural speech.
- Talking with girlfriend (casual register) and with her parents
  (polite / honorific registers) -- both directions: understanding them and
  speaking appropriately to them.

## Preferences
- Reading comprehension first (his strength), then low-typing production.
- Select-don't-type drills; phone keyboard friction is real.
- Full glosses available during drills so effort stays on grammar.
- Skip material he already knows; follow his pace, not lesson order.
- Content: slice-of-life and folktales; moderate grammar density, light
  vocab load. Natural, not textbook-y. Parallel Korean-Japanese format welcome.
- Dislikes: streaks, visible flashcard grind, content far above or below level.

## Immersion context
- Watches Korean dramas (Viki preferred), shadowing daily.
- Shadowing method: same 60-90 s passage 15-20x over several days.
